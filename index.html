About
Infinite voxel sandbox in one HTML file.

· Streaming chunks — the world builds
  itself as you explore, far chunks unload
· Flowing water & lava, falling sand,
  ladders, ice, glass, ores
· Day / night cycle with moving,
  shadow-casting clouds
· GLSL wind-shader Nigerian flag on the
  command tower at spawn
· Procedural 8-bit audio & piano score
· Zero external assets
